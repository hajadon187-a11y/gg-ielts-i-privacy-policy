English

1. Operator and contact

“Gachi Guild Flashcard for IELTS (GG IELTS)” is provided by Gachi Guild (“the Operator”).

Contact: gachiguild@gmail.com

2. Data handled by the app

The app is designed primarily for on-device learning. It does not require an account and does not send learning data to a server operated by the Operator. The app does not use advertising, analytics, or behavioral-tracking SDKs.

  • Vocabulary mastery status, favorites, and study history
  • Daily goals, study streaks, reminder settings, and app preferences
  • Custom vocabulary created by the user, where available

Depending on the user’s iOS device-backup settings, app data may be included in a backup managed by Apple.

3. Notifications

If notifications are allowed, the app may schedule a study reminder on the device when the daily goal has not been completed. Notification content is not sent to the Operator.

4. ChatGPT and Gemini

For optional AI conversation guidance, the app may try to open ChatGPT or Gemini using an iOS app link. If it cannot open the app, it opens an App Store search page. This result is handled on the device and is not sent to the Operator.

The app does not automatically send learning data to those services. Information entered into an external AI app is handled under that service’s privacy policy and terms.

5. Speech

The app uses iOS speech synthesis for pronunciation and example playback. Voice availability, any voice downloads, and other system-level processing are controlled by iOS and Apple and may depend on the device’s settings. Speech text is not sent to the Operator.

6. App Store purchases

StoreKit is used for premium features. Apple processes payment and payment-account information. The Operator does not receive card numbers or other payment details. The app receives purchase-status information needed to verify a purchase and unlock features. Apple’s handling of purchase data is subject to Apple’s privacy policy and terms.

7. Backup and restore

When the user explicitly creates a backup, the app exports vocabulary, study history, favorites, goals, streaks, and selected preferences to a location chosen through the iOS file picker.

Backup files are GZIP-compressed but are not encrypted by the app. Users should review the privacy and security practices of any file, cloud, or backup service they choose. Restore occurs only when the user selects a backup file.

8. Sharing, retention, and deletion

The Operator does not sell or provide learning data to advertising or analytics services. Learning data remains in the app’s storage on the device until the app is deleted. Copies included in a device backup may remain according to the user’s Apple backup settings. The user must delete exported backup files from their selected storage location.

The app has no Operator-managed account or server-side user data, so there is no separate account-deletion procedure.

9. Security and changes

Data is stored using iOS app storage and device mechanisms. The Operator cannot eliminate all risks from device loss, a compromised device, malicious software, or sharing backup files.

This policy may be updated when laws, App Store requirements, or app features change. The updated date will be changed when material changes are made.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, deletion requests, or other inquiries, contact gachiguild@gmail.com.

データはiOSのアプリ用ストレージおよび端末の仕組みを利用して保存します。ただし、端末の紛失、端末の不正な改変、悪意のあるソフトウェア、バックアップファイルの共有などのリスクを完全には排除できません。
